Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

Last Updated

This Privacy Policy was last updated on May 2026.

Who We Are

Qatar Live Weather (qatarliveweather.com) is an independent weather information service providing real-time weather data for Qatar and its municipalities. The site is operated by mohammedlglg. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Qatar Meteorology Department (QMD).

Data We Collect

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our own servers. However, the following third-party services operate on this site and may collect data subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics 4 — collects anonymized usage data such as pages visited, time on site, device type, browser, and general geographic region (country/city level). This helps us understand how visitors use the site and which features are most useful. Data is processed by Google. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Google AdSense — serves advertisements on the main weather map page (index.html) only, and only after live weather content has loaded. AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized ads via Google Ad Settings →
  • Geolocation (optional, browser-only) — if you click "Use my location," your browser requests your GPS coordinates to find the nearest weather station. This data is processed entirely within your browser and is never transmitted to our servers.

Cookies

This site uses the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) — help us understand how visitors interact with the site. These are only set if you accept cookies via our cookie consent banner.
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense) — used to deliver relevant advertisements on the main map page. These are only set if you accept cookies via our cookie consent banner.
  • Functional cookies — we store your cookie consent preference in localStorage so we don't ask you repeatedly. This is a first-party functional storage item and does not track you across other websites.

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser's privacy settings. Declining cookies via our banner will prevent Analytics and AdSense cookies from being set.

Where Ads Appear

Google AdSense advertisements appear only on the main map page (index.html), and only after weather data has successfully loaded and is visible on screen. We do not serve ads on static pages (About, Privacy, Contact) or during the page loading state. This practice ensures ads are always shown alongside genuine publisher content, consistent with Google's AdSense programme policies.

Third-Party Data Sources

Weather data displayed on this site is sourced from the following third-party services. These services operate independently and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies:

  • wttr.in — provides current conditions, forecasts, and astronomy data (powered by WorldWeatherOnline).
  • Aladhan API — provides Islamic prayer times based on geographic coordinates.
  • Wikipedia REST API — provides informational summaries about locations under the CC BY-SA licence.

Disclaimer: Weather data on this site is provided for general informational purposes only and is not official data from the Qatar Meteorology Department (QMD). For official forecasts and weather warnings, visit met.gov.qa →

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR (EU/UK) or other applicable privacy regulations, including the right to:

  • Access data collected about you by third-party services
  • Request deletion of your data from those services
  • Opt out of data collection or personalised advertising
  • Withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser

To exercise these rights regarding Google's data collection, visit Google's Data & Privacy settings →. For other questions, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
