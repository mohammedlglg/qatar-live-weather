Data Corrections

Weather data on this site comes from wttr.in. If you notice consistently incorrect readings for a specific location, please let us know:

  • The location name and municipality
  • The incorrect reading you're seeing
  • The correct expected value (ideally from an official source)

We'll investigate whether the issue is with our coordinates or the upstream data provider.
